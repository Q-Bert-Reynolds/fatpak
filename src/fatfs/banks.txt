# bank_split.py: FatFs is too big for one bank. Small helpers called from everywhere go in ROM0,
# the write/mount side goes in B, everything else (read side, file API) goes in A.
ROM0: ld_16 ld_32 st_16 st_32 ld_clust st_clust __memcpy memset memcmp strchr dbc_1st dbc_2nd move_window
ROM0: disk_status disk_initialize disk_read disk_write disk_ioctl
B: mount_volume find_volume check_fs get_ldnumber f_mount f_lseek
B: f_write create_chain put_fat remove_chain sync_window sync_fs f_sync f_close
B: dir_clear dir_alloc dir_register
